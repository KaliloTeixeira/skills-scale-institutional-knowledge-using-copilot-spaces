# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Engineers

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Engineers ensure software quality through testing strategy, test case design, and validation. They verify that features meet acceptance criteria and maintain quality gates throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans for features and releases
- Create and maintain automated test suites
- Validate acceptance criteria and perform exploratory testing
- Report and track defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate with developers on testability and quality improvements
- Monitor quality metrics and test coverage

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Reduce testing cycle time through automation
- Provide fast, actionable feedback on quality issues

### Typical Communication
- Daily coordination with developers on testing blockers
- Test reports and quality metrics in sprint reviews
- Defect triage sessions and release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide architectural guidance, technical direction, and mentorship to the development team. They ensure technical decisions align with long-term goals and best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and approve major technical decisions
- Mentor developers and conduct code reviews
- Identify and address technical debt
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on feasibility and estimates

### Goals
- Maintain system scalability, reliability, and maintainability
- Foster technical excellence within the team
- Balance short-term delivery with long-term technical health

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design reviews and technical decision records
- Technical guidance in code reviews and pair programming
- Capacity planning discussions with Project Managers

---

## DevOps/SRE Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps/SRE Engineers build and maintain infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational tools. They ensure system reliability, performance, and efficient delivery workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and cloud resources
- Monitor system health, performance, and reliability
- Implement security controls and compliance measures
- Respond to incidents and conduct post-incident reviews
- Automate operational tasks and deployments

### Goals
- Maximize system uptime and reliability
- Reduce deployment friction and cycle time
- Improve observability and incident response

### Typical Communication
- On-call rotations and incident response coordination
- Infrastructure and deployment planning with teams
- Performance and reliability metrics reporting

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They gather requirements, analyze processes, and ensure solutions deliver measurable business value.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements
- Analyze and model business processes
- Translate business needs into functional specifications
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Support user acceptance testing
- Facilitate communication between business stakeholders and delivery teams

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real business needs
- Clarify ambiguity and reduce rework
- Enable data-driven business decisions

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops with stakeholders
- User stories and acceptance criteria documentation
- Business impact analysis and reporting

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are individuals or groups with interest or influence in project outcomes. They provide inputs, constraints, and approvals throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and requirements
- Review and approve project deliverables and changes
- Allocate resources and budget
- Make prioritization decisions
- Participate in demos and milestone reviews

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver expected business outcomes
- Maintain visibility into project progress and risks
- Approve resource allocation and key decisions

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Approval requests for scope changes and budget
- Escalation path for major decisions and risks

---

## Role Interactions

### Daily Collaboration
- **Developers ↔ QA Engineers**: Feature implementation and testing coordination
- **Developers ↔ Technical Leads**: Code reviews and technical guidance
- **DevOps ↔ Developers**: CI/CD pipeline and deployment support

### Weekly Collaboration
- **Project Managers ↔ Product Managers**: Roadmap alignment and prioritization
- **Project Managers ↔ Technical Leads**: Capacity planning and risk management
- **Business Analysts ↔ Product Managers**: Requirements refinement and validation

### Milestone Collaboration
- **Product Managers ↔ Stakeholders**: Demo presentations and feedback
- **Project Managers ↔ Stakeholders**: Status updates and approval requests
- **All roles ↔ Team**: Retrospectives and continuous improvement
